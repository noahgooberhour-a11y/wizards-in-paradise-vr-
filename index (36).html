Wizards in Paradise

Swing your arms to climb and leap through the arena, and cast spells at your friends. Find the code book beside you to join friends online.

  • Move: walk with the left thumbstick and jump with B/Y, or push and pull against the ground, platforms and pillars with your hands.
  • Map: squeeze your left grip to pull out your treasure map, squeeze again to put it away. The world is huge now, and every area you have not visited shows a question mark.
  • Biomes: tundra, snowy mountains, grasslands, deserts, jungles and swamps. Each one has its own enemies, and castles, villages, pyramids, temples, ruins and huts to explore. Every building has weapons inside.
  • Day and night: a full day lasts 10 minutes. Skeletons come out at night.
  • Backpack: you wear a backpack. Reach over your shoulder and squeeze a grip to take it off, and its menu opens in front of you. Touch an item with a finger and pull the trigger. Release near your back to put it on, or let go to drop it. Pull the trigger on a weapon (or any item) to put it straight into the pack. Equip a weapon from the menu, then swing it at enemies.
  • Snow: outside the burrow door, put a hand in the snow chest and hold the trigger to grab a snowball, let go to throw it.
  • Spells: there are 26 spells now (scroll the list with the right stick). Heal (40 HP, 10s recharge), Shield (4s of 75% less damage) and Blink (teleport 7 steps ahead) have recharge timers shown on their cards.
  • Cast: hold a trigger to fire your chosen spell. Spells cost mana, which refills over time. Outside the burrow door, put a hand in the broom basket and press the trigger to grab a broom, press the trigger again to get on it, squeeze a grip to fly where you look, and press the trigger with your hand at the basket to put it back. You have 110 health; each spell does different damage, and if you hit zero you respawn at the burrow.
  • Spells: every spell is on the big menu on your left hand. Point at one and pull the trigger.
  • Paradise Pearls: you start with 100, and earn 10 for every wizard you defeat, and pearls for every enemy you defeat in the wild. Spend them on shirts, hats, faces and capes in the cosmetics room at the back of your burrow.
  • PvP: in a room, other wizards’ spells hurt you and launch you into the air, and yours do the same to them.
  • Name: tap NAME on the code book to pick your wizard name. Friends see it above your head.
