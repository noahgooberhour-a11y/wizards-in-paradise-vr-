Wizards in Paradise

Swing your arms to climb and leap through the arena, and cast spells at your friends. Find the code book beside you to join friends online.

  • Move: push your hands against the ground, platforms and pillars, then let go to launch.
  • Cast: hold a trigger to fire your chosen spell. Spells cost mana, which refills over time. Walk with the left thumbstick, jump with B/Y, or touch the ground with a hand and pull. Outside the burrow door, put a hand in the broom basket and press the trigger to grab a broom, press the trigger again to fly (steer with the left thumbstick, look where you want to go), and press the trigger with your hand at the basket to put it back. You have 110 health; each spell does different damage, and if you hit zero you respawn at the burrow.
  • Spells: every spell is on the big menu on your left hand. Point at one and pull the trigger.
  • Paradise Pearls: you start with 100, and earn 10 for every wizard you defeat. Spend them on shirts, hats, faces and capes in the cosmetics room at the back of your burrow.
  • PvP: in a room, other wizards’ spells hurt you and launch you into the air, and yours do the same to them.
  • Name: tap NAME on the code book to pick your wizard name. Friends see it above your head.
